Earnings Call Transcript - Infosys (INFY.NS) - Q4 FY2024

[MANAGEMENT] Our results this quarter reflect the strength of our diversified portfolio. Attrition has stabilised and we are confident in our talent pipeline. Margins were under pressure due to elevated input costs and wage inflation. Revenue growth was driven by strong deal wins and healthy client additions. We added 22 new clients this quarter. We think it might perhaps be reasonable to expect some improvement, but this is not certain.
[MANAGEMENT] Forward guidance: We are raising our full-year margin guidance to 10.4-15.8%, reflecting continued operating efficiency. For the coming year, we expect revenue growth of 9.2-18.0%, an increase from our prior guidance.

Q&A Session
[ANALYST] How are you thinking about debt levels and capital allocation going forward?
[MANAGEMENT] Growth has been softer than we would like, driven by delayed client decision-making, and we are watching this closely before committing to a stronger outlook.
[ANALYST] Can you give more colour on hiring plans and wage inflation?
[MANAGEMENT] We plan to continue investing in capacity, and our capex plans remain unchanged, focused on high-return areas.
[ANALYST] Any update on regulatory developments from SEBI or RBI that could affect the business?
[MANAGEMENT] Growth this quarter was broad based across geographies and verticals, and we expect similar momentum to continue into the next two quarters.
[ANALYST] What is your outlook on revenue growth for the next two quarters?
[MANAGEMENT] Growth this quarter was broad based across geographies and verticals, and we expect similar momentum to continue into the next two quarters.
[ANALYST] How should we think about capex plans given the current demand environment?
