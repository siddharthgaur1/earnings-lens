Earnings Call Transcript - Tata Consultancy Services (TCS.NS) - Q1 FY2025

[MANAGEMENT] Our results this quarter reflect the strength of our diversified portfolio. Margins expanded on the back of operating leverage and disciplined cost control. Attrition remains a challenge and we are investing in retention. Margins were under pressure due to elevated input costs and wage inflation. Revenue grew -1.9% year on year to Rs 40221 crore. We think it might perhaps be reasonable to expect some improvement, but this is not certain.
[MANAGEMENT] Forward guidance: We expect growth to be broadly in line with this year, in the 6.2-11.2% range.

Q&A Session
[ANALYST] What is your outlook on revenue growth for the next two quarters?
[MANAGEMENT] That's an important area for us and something the leadership team discusses regularly as part of our broader strategic priorities.
[ANALYST] Any update on regulatory developments from SEBI or RBI that could affect the business?
[MANAGEMENT] Sure, happy to address that. Margin improvement this quarter came from a mix of operating leverage, pricing discipline, and lower attrition-related costs, and we expect this to sustain.
[ANALYST] How are you thinking about debt levels and capital allocation going forward?
[MANAGEMENT] We are being more conservative on capex given the uncertain demand backdrop, and will reassess as the year progresses.
[ANALYST] Can you give more colour on hiring plans and wage inflation?
[MANAGEMENT] There are a lot of moving parts here, and we'd rather not get into specifics on a call like this.
[ANALYST] Could you comment on competitive intensity in your core markets?
[MANAGEMENT] That's a fair question. Margins were impacted by a few one-off costs this quarter, and while we expect some normalisation, visibility remains limited.
