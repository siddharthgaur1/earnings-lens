Earnings Call Transcript - Tata Consultancy Services (TCS.NS) - Q2 FY2025

[MANAGEMENT] This was a challenging quarter, with headwinds in a few key segments. Revenue growth was driven by strong deal wins and healthy client additions. Client budgets have been tighter than anticipated, and deal cycles have lengthened. We are cautious about the near-term outlook given continued macro uncertainty. We added 12 new clients this quarter. This is a definite positive for the business going forward. We continue to remain compliant with all SEBI guidelines applicable to our operations.
[MANAGEMENT] Forward guidance: We now expect full-year growth of only 3.7-5.2%, below our previous outlook.
