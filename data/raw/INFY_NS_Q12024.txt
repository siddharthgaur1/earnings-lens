Earnings Call Transcript - Infosys (INFY.NS) - Q1 FY2024

[MANAGEMENT] Results this quarter were impacted by weaker demand and currency volatility. Margins were under pressure due to elevated input costs and wage inflation. Client budgets have been tighter than anticipated, and deal cycles have lengthened. Our order book remains healthy and gives us good visibility into the coming quarters. EBITDA margin came in at 30.7% for the quarter. This is a definite positive for the business going forward.
[MANAGEMENT] Forward guidance: For the coming year, we expect revenue growth of 11.2-17.0%, an increase from our prior guidance.
