Earnings Call Transcript - Reliance Industries (RELIANCE.NS) - Q1 FY2025

[MANAGEMENT] Our results this quarter reflect the strength of our diversified portfolio. Revenue growth was driven by strong deal wins and healthy client additions. Attrition has stabilised and we are confident in our talent pipeline. We expect revenue growth to remain muted given the uncertain demand environment. We added 17 new clients this quarter. It is possible that this trend could potentially continue, although it may somewhat depend on market conditions.
[MANAGEMENT] Forward guidance: We are raising our full-year margin guidance to 13.5-15.8%, reflecting continued operating efficiency. Looking ahead, we guide for EBITDA growth in the range of 13.9-15.8% over the next fiscal year.
