Earnings Call Transcript - HDFC Bank (HDFCBANK.NS) - Q3 FY2024

[MANAGEMENT] It has been a mixed quarter, and we are watching the environment closely. Margins were under pressure due to elevated input costs and wage inflation. We expect revenue growth to remain muted given the uncertain demand environment. Attrition has stabilised and we are confident in our talent pipeline. EBITDA margin came in at 12.3% for the quarter. We are confident this trend will continue.
[MANAGEMENT] Forward guidance: We are revising our revenue growth guidance down to 2.3-8.9% for the full year.

Q&A Session
[ANALYST] Can you give more colour on hiring plans and wage inflation?
[MANAGEMENT] We are being more conservative on capex given the uncertain demand backdrop, and will reassess as the year progresses.
[ANALYST] What's driving the change in your guidance versus last quarter?
[MANAGEMENT] We plan to continue investing in capacity, and our capex plans remain unchanged, focused on high-return areas.
[ANALYST] Any update on regulatory developments from SEBI or RBI that could affect the business?
[MANAGEMENT] We are being more conservative on capex given the uncertain demand backdrop, and will reassess as the year progresses.
[ANALYST] Could you comment on competitive intensity in your core markets?
[MANAGEMENT] That's an important area for us and something the leadership team discusses regularly as part of our broader strategic priorities.
[ANALYST] Can you help us understand the drivers behind this quarter's margin performance?
[MANAGEMENT] Growth has been softer than we would like, driven by delayed client decision-making, and we are watching this closely before committing to a stronger outlook.
