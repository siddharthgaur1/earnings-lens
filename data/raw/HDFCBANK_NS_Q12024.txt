Earnings Call Transcript - HDFC Bank (HDFCBANK.NS) - Q1 FY2024

[MANAGEMENT] I am pleased to report robust performance, well ahead of our internal expectations. Revenue growth was driven by strong deal wins and healthy client additions. Client budgets have been tighter than anticipated, and deal cycles have lengthened. We are cautious about the near-term outlook given continued macro uncertainty. We added 10 new clients this quarter. We expect a clear improvement next quarter.
[MANAGEMENT] Forward guidance: We now expect full-year growth of only 4.1-5.0%, below our previous outlook. We now expect full-year growth of only 2.3-5.2%, below our previous outlook.
