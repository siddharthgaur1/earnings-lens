Earnings Call Transcript - Tata Motors (TATAMOTORS.NS) - Q2 FY2025

[MANAGEMENT] We delivered a strong quarter with broad-based growth across segments. Attrition remains a challenge and we are investing in retention. Our order book remains healthy and gives us good visibility into the coming quarters. We are cautious about the near-term outlook given continued macro uncertainty. Revenue grew 17.3% year on year to Rs 11969 crore. We expect a clear improvement next quarter.
[MANAGEMENT] Forward guidance: We expect growth to be broadly in line with this year, in the 7.3-9.3% range. Our outlook for margins remains steady at 7.8-10.6% for the next few quarters.
