Earnings Call Transcript - HDFC Bank (HDFCBANK.NS) - Q1 FY2025

[MANAGEMENT] Our results this quarter reflect the strength of our diversified portfolio. Attrition has stabilised and we are confident in our talent pipeline. We expect revenue growth to remain muted given the uncertain demand environment. We are cautious about the near-term outlook given continued macro uncertainty. Revenue grew 9.4% year on year to Rs 22435 crore. We think it might perhaps be reasonable to expect some improvement, but this is not certain.
[MANAGEMENT] Forward guidance: We now expect full-year growth of only 0.5-7.5%, below our previous outlook.

Q&A Session
[ANALYST] Can you help us understand the drivers behind this quarter's margin performance?
[MANAGEMENT] Growth this quarter was broad based across geographies and verticals, and we expect similar momentum to continue into the next two quarters.
[ANALYST] How should we think about capex plans given the current demand environment?
[MANAGEMENT] That's a fair question. Margins were impacted by a few one-off costs this quarter, and while we expect some normalisation, visibility remains limited.
[ANALYST] Could you comment on competitive intensity in your core markets?
[MANAGEMENT] There are a lot of moving parts here, and we'd rather not get into specifics on a call like this.
[ANALYST] Any update on regulatory developments from SEBI or RBI that could affect the business?
[MANAGEMENT] We plan to continue investing in capacity, and our capex plans remain unchanged, focused on high-return areas.
[ANALYST] How are you thinking about debt levels and capital allocation going forward?
[MANAGEMENT] Growth has been softer than we would like, driven by delayed client decision-making, and we are watching this closely before committing to a stronger outlook.
