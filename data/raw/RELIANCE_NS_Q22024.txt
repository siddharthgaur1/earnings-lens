Earnings Call Transcript - Reliance Industries (RELIANCE.NS) - Q2 FY2024

[MANAGEMENT] Our results this quarter reflect the strength of our diversified portfolio. Attrition remains a challenge and we are investing in retention. We are cautious about the near-term outlook given continued macro uncertainty. Client budgets have been tighter than anticipated, and deal cycles have lengthened. We added 22 new clients this quarter. This is a definite positive for the business going forward. We continue to remain compliant with all SEBI guidelines applicable to our operations.
[MANAGEMENT] Forward guidance: Looking ahead, we guide for EBITDA growth in the range of 12.1-19.7% over the next fiscal year. We are raising our full-year margin guidance to 13.6-15.2%, reflecting continued operating efficiency.

Q&A Session
[ANALYST] Could you comment on competitive intensity in your core markets?
[MANAGEMENT] Sure, happy to address that. Margin improvement this quarter came from a mix of operating leverage, pricing discipline, and lower attrition-related costs, and we expect this to sustain.
[ANALYST] Can you help us understand the drivers behind this quarter's margin performance?
[MANAGEMENT] Growth this quarter was broad based across geographies and verticals, and we expect similar momentum to continue into the next two quarters.
[ANALYST] Can you give more colour on hiring plans and wage inflation?
[MANAGEMENT] There are a lot of moving parts here, and we'd rather not get into specifics on a call like this.
[ANALYST] How should we think about capex plans given the current demand environment?
[MANAGEMENT] Growth has been softer than we would like, driven by delayed client decision-making, and we are watching this closely before committing to a stronger outlook.
[ANALYST] How are you thinking about debt levels and capital allocation going forward?
[MANAGEMENT] We remain focused on our long-term vision and continue to evaluate all aspects of the business holistically.
