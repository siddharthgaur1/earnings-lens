Earnings Call Transcript - Infosys (INFY.NS) - Q3 FY2024

[MANAGEMENT] Results this quarter were impacted by weaker demand and currency volatility. We are cautious about the near-term outlook given continued macro uncertainty. Margins were under pressure due to elevated input costs and wage inflation. Revenue growth was driven by strong deal wins and healthy client additions. EBITDA margin came in at 21.2% for the quarter. We are confident this trend will continue.
[MANAGEMENT] Forward guidance: We expect growth to be broadly in line with this year, in the 6.1-9.6% range.

Q&A Session
[ANALYST] Can you give more colour on hiring plans and wage inflation?
[MANAGEMENT] We plan to continue investing in capacity, and our capex plans remain unchanged, focused on high-return areas.
[ANALYST] How should we think about capex plans given the current demand environment?
[MANAGEMENT] Growth this quarter was broad based across geographies and verticals, and we expect similar momentum to continue into the next two quarters.
