Earnings Call Transcript - Tata Consultancy Services (TCS.NS) - Q1 FY2024

[MANAGEMENT] This has been one of our best quarters in recent years, and momentum is clearly building. Revenue growth was driven by strong deal wins and healthy client additions. Attrition remains a challenge and we are investing in retention. Our order book remains healthy and gives us good visibility into the coming quarters. Revenue grew 1.1% year on year to Rs 36917 crore. We are confident this trend will continue.
[MANAGEMENT] Forward guidance: Our outlook for margins remains steady at 8.4-9.0% for the next few quarters. We expect growth to be broadly in line with this year, in the 8.1-10.0% range.
