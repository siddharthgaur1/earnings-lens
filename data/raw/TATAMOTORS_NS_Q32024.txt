Earnings Call Transcript - Tata Motors (TATAMOTORS.NS) - Q3 FY2024

[MANAGEMENT] This was a challenging quarter, with headwinds in a few key segments. Client budgets have been tighter than anticipated, and deal cycles have lengthened. We expect revenue growth to remain muted given the uncertain demand environment. Attrition has stabilised and we are confident in our talent pipeline. We added 32 new clients this quarter. There could be some upside, though it is difficult to say with confidence at this stage.
[MANAGEMENT] Forward guidance: We are revising our revenue growth guidance down to 4.0-5.5% for the full year. We now expect full-year growth of only 3.3-8.1%, below our previous outlook.
