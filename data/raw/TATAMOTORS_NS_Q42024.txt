Earnings Call Transcript - Tata Motors (TATAMOTORS.NS) - Q4 FY2024

[MANAGEMENT] We delivered a strong quarter with broad-based growth across segments. Our order book remains healthy and gives us good visibility into the coming quarters. Revenue growth was driven by strong deal wins and healthy client additions. Attrition remains a challenge and we are investing in retention. We added 9 new clients this quarter. We are confident this trend will continue.
[MANAGEMENT] Forward guidance: For the coming year, we expect revenue growth of 12.6-17.4%, an increase from our prior guidance. We are raising our full-year margin guidance to 13.1-19.8%, reflecting continued operating efficiency.
