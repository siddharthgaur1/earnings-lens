Earnings Call Transcript - Reliance Industries (RELIANCE.NS) - Q4 FY2024

[MANAGEMENT] It has been a mixed quarter, and we are watching the environment closely. Attrition remains a challenge and we are investing in retention. We are cautious about the near-term outlook given continued macro uncertainty. We continue to see strong demand in our core segments and are scaling capacity accordingly. Revenue grew 2.9% year on year to Rs 45087 crore. This is a definite positive for the business going forward. We continue to remain compliant with all TRAI guidelines applicable to our operations.
[MANAGEMENT] Forward guidance: Given the current environment, we expect margins to be in the range of 3.6-8.9%, lower than earlier guided. We now expect full-year growth of only 3.0-8.9%, below our previous outlook.
