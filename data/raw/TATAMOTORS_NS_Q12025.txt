Earnings Call Transcript - Tata Motors (TATAMOTORS.NS) - Q1 FY2025

[MANAGEMENT] We delivered a strong quarter with broad-based growth across segments. Margins were under pressure due to elevated input costs and wage inflation. Attrition has stabilised and we are confident in our talent pipeline. Revenue growth was driven by strong deal wins and healthy client additions. EBITDA margin came in at 17.5% for the quarter. This is a definite positive for the business going forward. We continue to remain compliant with all RBI guidelines applicable to our operations.
[MANAGEMENT] Forward guidance: We are raising our full-year margin guidance to 12.1-17.6%, reflecting continued operating efficiency.

Q&A Session
[ANALYST] What's driving the change in your guidance versus last quarter?
[MANAGEMENT] We remain focused on our long-term vision and continue to evaluate all aspects of the business holistically.
[ANALYST] Can you help us understand the drivers behind this quarter's margin performance?
[MANAGEMENT] Growth has been softer than we would like, driven by delayed client decision-making, and we are watching this closely before committing to a stronger outlook.
[ANALYST] What is your outlook on revenue growth for the next two quarters?
[MANAGEMENT] Growth this quarter was broad based across geographies and verticals, and we expect similar momentum to continue into the next two quarters.
[ANALYST] Could you comment on competitive intensity in your core markets?
[MANAGEMENT] Growth this quarter was broad based across geographies and verticals, and we expect similar momentum to continue into the next two quarters.
