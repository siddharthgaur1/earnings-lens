Earnings Call Transcript - Tata Motors (TATAMOTORS.NS) - Q1 FY2024

[MANAGEMENT] It has been a mixed quarter, and we are watching the environment closely. Margins were under pressure due to elevated input costs and wage inflation. We expect revenue growth to remain muted given the uncertain demand environment. Attrition remains a challenge and we are investing in retention. Revenue grew 11.2% year on year to Rs 14602 crore. We think it might perhaps be reasonable to expect some improvement, but this is not certain.
[MANAGEMENT] Forward guidance: We now expect full-year growth of only 1.8-8.6%, below our previous outlook.

Q&A Session
[ANALYST] Can you give more colour on hiring plans and wage inflation?
[MANAGEMENT] Growth has been softer than we would like, driven by delayed client decision-making, and we are watching this closely before committing to a stronger outlook.
[ANALYST] Can you help us understand the drivers behind this quarter's margin performance?
[MANAGEMENT] That's an important area for us and something the leadership team discusses regularly as part of our broader strategic priorities.
[ANALYST] Could you comment on competitive intensity in your core markets?
[MANAGEMENT] That's a fair question. Margins were impacted by a few one-off costs this quarter, and while we expect some normalisation, visibility remains limited.
[ANALYST] How should we think about capex plans given the current demand environment?
[MANAGEMENT] We remain focused on our long-term vision and continue to evaluate all aspects of the business holistically.
[ANALYST] What is your outlook on revenue growth for the next two quarters?
[MANAGEMENT] We plan to continue investing in capacity, and our capex plans remain unchanged, focused on high-return areas.
