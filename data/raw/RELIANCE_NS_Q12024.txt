Earnings Call Transcript - Reliance Industries (RELIANCE.NS) - Q1 FY2024

[MANAGEMENT] We navigated a difficult macro environment, and performance was largely in line with a soft market. We continue to see strong demand in our core segments and are scaling capacity accordingly. Revenue growth was driven by strong deal wins and healthy client additions. Our order book remains healthy and gives us good visibility into the coming quarters. Revenue grew 17.6% year on year to Rs 32979 crore. We think it might perhaps be reasonable to expect some improvement, but this is not certain.
[MANAGEMENT] Forward guidance: For the coming year, we expect revenue growth of 11.9-14.8%, an increase from our prior guidance.
