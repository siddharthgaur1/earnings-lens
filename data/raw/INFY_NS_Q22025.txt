Earnings Call Transcript - Infosys (INFY.NS) - Q2 FY2025

[MANAGEMENT] It has been a mixed quarter, and we are watching the environment closely. Margins expanded on the back of operating leverage and disciplined cost control. Client budgets have been tighter than anticipated, and deal cycles have lengthened. Attrition remains a challenge and we are investing in retention. EBITDA margin came in at 16.7% for the quarter. We are confident this trend will continue. We continue to remain compliant with all TRAI guidelines applicable to our operations.
[MANAGEMENT] Forward guidance: For the coming year, we expect revenue growth of 8.9-16.3%, an increase from our prior guidance.
